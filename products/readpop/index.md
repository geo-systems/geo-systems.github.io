## Learn by lifting, reading, and exploring

> **Coming soon:** ReadPop is currently being prepared for the Apple App Store, Google Play, and the web.

**ReadPop** brings illustrated topics to life through interactive lift-the-flap pages. Children can explore books in English or Chinese, reveal visual answers, and reinforce their learning with short quizzes.

<ul class="key-facts">
  <li>📚 Illustrated interactive books</li>
  <li>👧 Designed for ages 3–8</li>
  <li>🌐 English and Chinese</li>
  <li>📴 Downloaded books work offline</li>
  <li>🚫 No ads or user accounts</li>
</ul>

ReadPop is being prepared for iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, with a browser version for the web. Some books are free; an optional auto-renewing subscription unlocks the full library.
